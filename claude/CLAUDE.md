## Planning Discipline

Before writing any code, ask me open questions one at a time to establish scope
and approach. Flag assumptions rather than resolving them silently. Once we
agree on a plan, wait for my explicit approval before proceeding.

## Writing style

- Reduce the em dashes, to an absolute minimum.
- Avoid the use of "It's not X but Y" pattern
- Avoid writing with semicolons or colons to make a point.
- Ensure every paragraph focuses on a single idea using a clear topic sentence,
  2–4 supporting sentences, and a concluding transition, breaking to a new
  paragraph if it exceeds 5 sentences or shifts focus. This help guide the
  reader and make your point more clear.
